The bush medicine research was undertaken as a part of the Central Land Council
(CLC) ranger program. Research was conducted by Philip Jimmy Yanypa Japalyi, Paddy
Doolak Jangari, Helma Bernard Nalyirri, George Sambo Jangala, Nikita Smiler Nangala,
Harlem Tanami Janama-Jurlama, Cassandra Algy Nimarra, Andre Marais and Felicity
Meakins. Gurindji information was provided by Violet Wadrill Nanaku and Biddy
Wavehill Yamawurr.

Photographs were provided by Penny Smith, Peggy Macqueen, Brenda L Croft and Glenn
Wightman, and are copyright by the photographers and
reproduced with permission. Website created by Ben Foley and Abigail Davis.

The work and publication costs were funded by the CLC. This project was also
supported by Karungkarni Art, the School of Languages and Cultures at the University
of Queensland and the ARC Centre of Excellence for the Dynamics of Language, and
relied heavily on previous botanical identification work by Glenn Wightman.
